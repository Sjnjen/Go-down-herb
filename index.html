The Pot of Life

Go Down Herbs (PTY) Ltd is rooted in Venda tradition, prepared using ancestral recipes passed down through generations. Founded by a traditional healer with deep community ties, every product is handcrafted while honouring nature and heritage. We believe in sharing the Pot of Life with the world, blending ancient wisdom with modern wellness.

Founder: Makhadzi Ramabulana

Heritage: Venda traditional healing since 1998

Community: Supporting local farmers and gatherers
