Who we are

Introduction Of Who We Are

Go-Down Herbs is a South African-based company specializing in the production, distribution and sales of a unique herbal mixture drinks/beverages under the brand name Go-Down Herbs.

The herbal mixture drinks are designed to promote health and wellness to consumers. The company positions itself as a leading provider of herbal health solutions, catering to health-conscious consumers seeking natural remedies.

By leveraging traditional knowledge and modern production techniques, Go-Down Herbs aims to deliver high-quality, effective, and affordable herbal health products.

As a trusted provider of herbal health solutions, the company is committed to promoting natural wellness through innovative and high-quality herbal beverages.

Our story

About Us

"Go Down Herbs" an African traditional Venda herbs that has been in 3 family generations now.

From a late granny, to father, now to the kids — a family of healers and farmers.

It is a natural herb made by believers (Seventh Day Adventist Church) for both Christians and non-believers, for the young ones and elders, male or female African people.

Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before ordering from Go Down Herbs (PTY) Ltd.

Payment Terms

All orders must be paid in full at the time of checkout. Payments are processed securely through Paystack. We do not store or have access to your card details.

Delivery Information

Orders are dispatched via The Courier Guy, PostNet, PUDO Locker, or Bolt/Uber depending on the option selected at checkout. Delivery times vary by location and courier availability. Please note that liquid products cannot be sent via PEP/Paxi.

Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and complete delivery details at checkout.
  • Ensure someone is available to receive the delivery at the address provided.
  • Contact us promptly via WhatsApp if there is an issue with your order.

Product Usage Information

Our products are traditional Venda herbal preparations. They are not medically tested, evaluated, or approved. Always follow the instructions provided with each product and consult a healthcare professional if you have any medical concerns.

Privacy Information

Your personal information (name, phone number, email and delivery address) is collected only to process and deliver your order and will not be shared with third parties beyond what is required for payment processing and delivery.

Order Processing

Orders are processed once payment has been confirmed. You will be contacted via the phone number or email provided if there are any issues with your order.

Returns

No returns are accepted at this time unless the product received is incorrect or damaged.
